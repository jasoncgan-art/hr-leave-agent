# Sick Leave — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-01-15. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice.

[Section: SICK-1] Accrual. Full-time employees accrue 1 sick day per calendar
month of service, up to a maximum of 12 sick days per year. Unused sick days
carry over as described in the carryover policy.

[Section: SICK-2] Permitted uses. Sick leave may be used for the employee's
own illness, injury, medical appointments, or to care for an ill immediate
family member. Employees must notify their manager before the start of the
workday when using sick leave.

[Section: SICK-3] No payout. Accrued but unused sick leave is not paid out at
separation and cannot be converted to PTO.

[Section: SICK-4] Documentation. For absences of 3 or more consecutive sick
days, a note from a licensed healthcare provider may be requested.
