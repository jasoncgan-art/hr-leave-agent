# Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-01-15. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice.

[Section: PTO-1] Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible to accrue
Paid Time Off (PTO) beginning on their hire date. PTO may be used for vacation,
personal days, or any other personal reason. Part-time employees and interns
do not accrue PTO under this policy.

[Section: PTO-2] Accrual rates by tenure. Employees accrue PTO each pay period
according to completed years of service: less than 2 years of service accrue
10 days per year (0.83 days per month); 2 to 5 years of service accrue 15 days
per year (1.25 days per month); 5 or more years of service accrue 20 days per
year (1.67 days per month).

[Section: PTO-3] Probationary period. During the first 90 days of employment
(the probationary period), employees accrue PTO but may not use it. After day
90, all accrued PTO becomes available for use.

[Section: PTO-4] Scheduling. PTO requests should be submitted at least 14
calendar days in advance through the leave-ticket system. Managers approve
requests based on team coverage needs.
