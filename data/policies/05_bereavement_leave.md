# Bereavement Leave — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-01-15. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice.

[Section: BER-1] Immediate family. Employees receive up to 5 paid days of
bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, defined as a
spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, or sibling.

[Section: BER-2] Extended family. Employees receive up to 2 paid days of
bereavement leave for the death of an extended family member, defined as a
grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, or sibling-in-law.

[Section: BER-3] Additional time. Additional unpaid time off for bereavement
may be requested under the unpaid leave policy with manager approval.
