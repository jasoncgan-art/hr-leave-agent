# Unpaid Leave — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-02-01. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice.

[Section: UPL-1] General rule. After all applicable paid leave is exhausted,
employees may request up to 30 calendar days of unpaid leave per rolling
12-month period, subject to manager and HR approval. Requests are evaluated
based on business needs and the employee's circumstances.

[Section: UPL-2] Benefits. Health benefits continue during approved unpaid
leave, but the employee remains responsible for their normal premium share.
PTO and sick leave do not accrue during unpaid leave lasting more than 5
consecutive days.
