# Leave Carryover — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-01-15. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice.

[Section: CO-1] PTO carryover. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO
days into the next calendar year. Carried-over PTO must be used by March 31;
any remaining carried-over days are forfeited on April 1. PTO above the 5-day
cap is forfeited at year end ("use it or lose it").

[Section: CO-2] Sick leave carryover. Unused sick days carry over in full into
the next calendar year, up to a maximum sick-leave bank of 40 days. Once the
40-day cap is reached, no further sick days accrue until the balance drops
below the cap.
