# Family Medical Leave — Acme Corp (fictional)

Last updated: 2026-02-01. This document is fully synthetic and written for a
software demo. It is not real legal or HR advice. This policy is modeled on
the U.S. Family and Medical Leave Act but is a fictional company policy.

[Section: FML-1] Entitlement. Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of
job-protected, unpaid family medical leave in a rolling 12-month period for:
the birth or adoption of a child, care for a spouse/child/parent with a
serious health condition, or the employee's own serious health condition.

[Section: FML-2] Eligibility. Employees are eligible after 12 months of
service and at least 1,250 hours worked in the preceding 12 months.

[Section: FML-3] Intermittent leave. Leave may be taken intermittently or on a
reduced schedule when medically necessary, or for birth/adoption with manager
approval.

[Section: FML-4] Notice. Employees must provide 30 days' advance notice when
the need for leave is foreseeable, or notice as soon as practicable otherwise.
